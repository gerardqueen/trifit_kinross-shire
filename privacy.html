Privacy

This page explains what this website does with your information.

The enquiry form

When you send an enquiry we receive what you type into the form: your name, email address, phone number if you give one, what you are interested in, and your message. It is emailed to TRI FIT Kinross-shire and kept in the website's database, where only the gym's website admins can read it. We use it to reply to you and for nothing else.

Booking and memberships

Classes and memberships are booked and paid for through TeamUp, which has its own terms and privacy policy. This website does not take payments.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts and stores a copy of the page content in your browser so that the site opens faster next time.

Your details

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you from this website, contact us using the details on the enquiry section.
